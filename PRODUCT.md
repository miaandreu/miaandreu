## Users

- **Engineering recruiters and hiring engineers** at MEP / energy / building-systems firms (WSP-type consultancies) evaluating an NYU mechanical engineering student (the site does not advertise that she is seeking internships; user direction 2026-09-24). Usually arrive from a resume or LinkedIn link, skim fast, and want evidence of real technical work.
- **Grad school and fellowship reviewers** (admissions committees, scholarship and research-program panels) who read more slowly and weigh trajectory, global experience, service, and a coherent research interest.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio of Mia Andreu, B.S. Mechanical Engineering at NYU Tandon (expected May 2027), framed around two UN Sustainable Development Goals: SDG 11, Sustainable Cities and Communities, and SDG 4, Quality Education (user direction, 2026-09-24). Energy work appears as evidence inside that frame, not as the headline. Success: a visitor digs into the project case studies and leaves convinced the work is real, technical, and hers. The CV download and contact are secondary actions.
